## 1. Overview

The Company operates sendit scan (senditscan.com), an AI-powered salvage auction analyzer for Copart and IAA listings. The Affiliate agrees to promote the Service to their audience in exchange for commission on referred paying subscribers.

## 2. Referral Tracking

- The Affiliate will be assigned a unique referral link: `https://senditscan.com/pricing?ref=vtuned`
- A referral is attributed to the Affiliate when a user visits the Service through the referral link and subscribes to a paid plan within **14 days** of clicking the link.
- If the user clicks the referral link again, the 14-day window resets.
- Each referred user can only be attributed to one affiliate.

## 3. Commission

- The Affiliate earns **20% recurring commission** on all payments made by referred users for as long as those users maintain an active paid subscription.
- Commission is calculated on the net amount received by the Company after payment processing fees (Stripe).
- Commission applies to all subscription tiers (Pro at $30/mo, Dealer at $120/mo) and any future tiers.

## 4. Promotional Discount

- Referred users receive **25% off their first month** of any paid plan.
- This discount is applied automatically when a user subscribes through the Affiliate's referral link.

## 5. Payouts

- Commissions are calculated monthly and paid within **15 days** of the end of each calendar month.
- Payments will be made via PayPal or Zelle to an account designated by the Affiliate.
- Minimum payout threshold: **$50**. If commissions are below $50 in a given month, the balance rolls over to the next month.
- The Affiliate can view their referral stats, click counts, signups, and estimated commissions via the dashboard provided by the Company.

## 6. Refunds and Chargebacks

- If a referred user requests a refund or initiates a chargeback within **30 days** of their initial payment, the commission for that transaction will be deducted from the Affiliate's next payout.
- Commissions are only earned on successfully completed, non-refunded payments.

## 7. Free Account

- The Affiliate receives a complimentary Pro account for the duration of this agreement at no cost.

## 8. Promotion Guidelines

- The Affiliate may promote the Service through YouTube videos, social media, blog posts, or other content at their discretion.
- There is no minimum number of promotions required.
- The Affiliate agrees not to make false or misleading claims about the Service's capabilities.
- The Affiliate agrees to disclose the affiliate relationship in accordance with FTC guidelines (e.g., "This video contains an affiliate link" or similar disclosure).

## 9. Non-Exclusivity

- This agreement is non-exclusive. The Affiliate is free to promote other products and services, including competing products.
- The Company is free to partner with other affiliates and creators.

## 10. Intellectual Property

- The Affiliate may use the Company's name, logo, and screenshots of the Service for promotional purposes.
- The Company may reference the Affiliate's name and channel in its own marketing materials.

## 11. Termination

- Either party may terminate this agreement with **30 days written notice** via email.
- Upon termination:
  - The Affiliate's referral link will be deactivated for new referrals.
  - The Affiliate will continue to earn commissions on **existing referred users for 6 months** following the termination date.
  - After the 6-month post-termination period, all commission obligations cease.
  - The Affiliate's free Pro account will be deactivated.

## 12. Liability

- The Company is not liable for any claims arising from the Affiliate's promotional content.
- The Affiliate is not liable for any issues with the Service, including downtime, bugs, or inaccurate analyses.

## 13. Modifications

- This agreement may be modified with mutual written consent of both parties via email.
- Changes to commission rates require 30 days notice to the Affiliate.
