Terms & Conditions

1. Referral Tracking

Referrals are tracked via a unique URL parameter (?ref=vtuned). Attribution is stored client-side for 14 days from the most recent click. If a user clicks the referral link multiple times, the window resets. Each referred user can only be attributed to one affiliate.

2. Commission

The Affiliate earns 20% recurring commission on all payments made by referred users for as long as those users maintain an active paid subscription. Commission is calculated on the net amount received by the Company after payment processing fees (Stripe: 2.9% + $0.30 per transaction). Commission applies to all current and future subscription tiers.

3. Refunds & Chargebacks

If a referred user requests a refund or initiates a chargeback within 30 days of their payment, the commission for that transaction will be deducted from the Affiliate's next payout. Commissions are only earned on successfully completed, non-refunded payments.

4. Payouts

Commissions are calculated monthly and paid within 15 days of the end of each calendar month via PayPal or Zelle. Minimum payout threshold is $50. Balances below $50 roll over to the following month. The Company will provide real-time referral stats including clicks, signups, conversions, and estimated commissions.

5. Promotion Guidelines

The Affiliate may promote the Service through YouTube videos, social media, blog posts, or other content at their discretion. There is no minimum number of promotions required. The Affiliate agrees not to make false or misleading claims about the Service and agrees to disclose the affiliate relationship in accordance with FTC guidelines (e.g., "This video contains an affiliate link" or equivalent disclosure).

6. Non-Exclusivity

This agreement is non-exclusive. The Affiliate is free to promote other products and services, including competing products. The Company is free to partner with other affiliates and creators.

7. Intellectual Property

The Affiliate may use the Company's name, logo, and screenshots of the Service for promotional purposes. The Company may reference the Affiliate's name and channel in its own marketing materials.

8. Termination

BY AFFILIATE: The Affiliate may terminate this agreement at any time with 30 days written notice via email, for any reason or no reason.

BY COMPANY: The Company may only terminate this agreement for cause, including but not limited to: (a) the Affiliate makes materially false or misleading claims about the Service; (b) the Affiliate engages in fraudulent referral activity; (c) the Affiliate violates FTC disclosure requirements after being notified; (d) the Service is permanently discontinued. The Company must provide written notice specifying the reason for termination and allow 30 days to cure the issue before termination takes effect (except in cases of fraud, which are effective immediately).

POST-TERMINATION COMMISSIONS: Regardless of which party terminates or the reason, the Affiliate will continue to earn commissions on all existing referred users for 6 months following the termination date. After the 6-month post-termination period, commission obligations cease. The complimentary Pro account will be deactivated 30 days after termination.

9. Liability

The Company is not liable for any claims arising from the Affiliate's promotional content. The Affiliate is not liable for any issues with the Service, including downtime, bugs, or inaccurate analyses. The Company's total liability shall not exceed the total commissions paid to the Affiliate in the 3 months preceding any claim.

10. Modifications & Governing Law

This agreement may be modified with mutual written consent via email. Changes to commission rates require 30 days notice to the Affiliate. This agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Texas.
